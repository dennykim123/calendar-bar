とくちょう · what's inside

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Like the Windows taskbar calendar, but native — and a lot cuter — on your Mac.

🗓️

Lives in your menu bar

One tap opens the month. Today is highlighted, and you can hop around with arrows, scroll, trackpad swipe, or the keyboard.

🎨

Make it yours

Show the date, weekday, or a tear-off calendar icon — in seven shapes and ten colors. Pick your language, too.

🔒

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no network. It never touches your calendars, contacts, or location. Everything stays on your Mac.

⚡

Light & native

Built with Swift & AppKit. Under 30 MB of memory, no Dock icon, and basically zero CPU while it waits.
